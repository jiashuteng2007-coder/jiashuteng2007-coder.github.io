This project studies the stability of ion crystals for trapped-ion quantum computing, combining optical-tweezer control with simulations that explicitly include radio-frequency micromotion. I calculate equilibrium configurations and collective vibrational modes, and develop adaptive tweezer-placement algorithms based on soft-mode participation.

I built a simulation workflow incorporating Coulomb interactions, Doppler cooling, and Gaussian optical-tweezer potentials to study collision-induced orbital deviations and cooling recovery in 300-ion crystals. My work includes modeling, algorithm implementation, numerical validation, and analysis of recovery under different trapping and tweezer conditions.

The project is ongoing; detailed methods and unpublished results are not publicly disclosed.
