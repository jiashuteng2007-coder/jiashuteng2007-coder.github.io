Highly reflective atomic mirrors make light–matter interactions inside an atomic cavity difficult to probe. This project investigates a two-waveguide scheme that uses an auxiliary waveguide to drive a probe atom, revealing the cavity’s collective modes and their interactions with the probe through optical spectra.

I carried out the analytical derivations, numerical calculations, figure preparation, and initial manuscript drafting. The manuscript is in preparation and has not yet been published.
